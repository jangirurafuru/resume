We’ve Been Discovering Japan Since 2005.
Now We’d Love to Share It With You.

2005年から始まった私たちの日本探訪 — あなただけの旅を

Japan has been part of our lives since 2005. What started as a journey to discover a new country slowly became something much more personal. Over the years, we’ve travelled its cities and countryside, wandered through quiet neighbourhoods, found little places that never make it into guidebooks, experienced local traditions, and most importantly fallen in love with the everyday Japan that exists beyond the famous sights.

“We’re not here simply to show you Japan’s most popular places. We want you to experience the Japan that made us fall in love with it.”

It might be a little street you would otherwise walk past, a family run restaurant tucked away in a quiet neighbourhood, a beautiful temple without a crowd or a local tradition you didn’t know about. These simple discoveries often become the memories people take home with them.

As vegetarians ourselves, food has always been an important part of how we experience Japan. Over the years, we’ve searched, tasted, explored, and discovered places where vegan /vegetarian food isn’t simply an afterthought. We know the restaurants worth travelling for, the hidden gems worth finding, and the experiences that can make a meal one of your favourite memories of Japan.

Because every traveller experiences Japan differently, we believe your journey should feel personal from the very beginning. Before planning your trip, we take the time to understand you and your interests, your pace, the food you enjoy and what you’re hoping to experience. Then we bring those details together to create a journey that feels right for you, rather than following a standard itinerary.

This company is built from something we have personally experienced for more than two decades rather than from a guidebook or a list of tourist attractions.

We know that choosing a travel company is about trust. You’re giving us a part of something very special: your time, your holiday, and your memories of Japan. And, We don’t take that lightly.

Whether you’re visiting Japan for the first time or returning to see a different side of the country, we’d love to help you create a journey that feels personal, meaningful and full of discoveries. Come and explore Japan with us not just as a visitor, but through the Japan we know and love.

Why Japan?

About Japan

A land where ancient traditions meet cutting-edge innovation. Here's what makes Japan truly unforgettable.

⛩️

Ancient Temples

寺院 — Jiin

Over 75,000 temples and shrines across Japan. From serene Zen gardens to golden pavilions — every visit is a journey through centuries of history.

🏙️

Modern Cities

都市 — Toshi

Neon-lit streets, robot restaurants, world-class shopping — Japan's cities are a mind-blowing fusion of the future and tradition.

🗻

Breathtaking Nature

自然 — Shizen

From the iconic Mount Fuji to bamboo forests and hot spring valleys — Japan's landscapes will take your breath away.

🍱

Incredible Food

食事 — Shokuji

Ramen, sushi, tempura, matcha — and amazing vegetarian & vegan options. Japan's food scene is a universe of flavors waiting to be explored.

🌸

Four Magical Seasons

四季 — Shiki

Cherry blossoms in spring, festivals in summer, fiery autumn foliage, and snow-dusted temples in winter. Every season is a new Japan.

Best Time to Visit

Japan's Four Seasons

Every season transforms Japan into a new experience. We'll help you pick the perfect time for your trip.

🌸

Spring

春 — Haru

March – May

Cherry blossom season. Mild weather, hanami picnics, and the most iconic Japan experience. The best time for first-time visitors.

🎆

Summer

夏 — Natsu

June – August

Vibrant festivals, fireworks, mountain hiking, and coastal adventures. Hot and humid but packed with energy and traditions.

🍁

Autumn

秋 — Aki

September – November

Fiery red and golden foliage paints every temple and garden. Perfect weather for hiking and exploring without the summer crowds.

❄️

Winter

冬 — Fuyu

December – February

Snow monkeys, ski resorts, illumination festivals, and the magic of snow-covered temples. Fewer tourists and cozy onsen experiences.
